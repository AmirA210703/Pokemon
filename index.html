Should I evolve
this Pokémon?

Almost every evolution makes the numbers bigger. The useful question is what you give up — speed, a resistance, an ability, or a choice you cannot take back. Look one up and find out.

    These four are where the answer is not simply “yes”.

    How this works. Every verdict is computed from the supplied pokemon.csv: the six base stats, both types, abilities and the evolves_from_species_id / evolution_chain_id columns that reconstruct each evolution line. Type matchups use the Generation VI chart. Artwork comes from the PokeAPI sprite set, matched on the url_image id.

    What it cannot tell you. The dataset has no levels, evolution methods, moves or held items. So it will not tell you that a Pokémon learns a move earlier unevolved, which stone you need, or what the trade requirements are. It answers the part the numbers can answer, and says so.
